214 voices for Ala
2025

The 10 Channel Spatial Sound Installation engages with the Kunstbau’s spatial characteristics by treating the concept of anger as a durational and process-based material. The title refers to the 214 voices generated through a granular synthesis instrument, with each voice embodying a variation of tone and intensity. By juxtaposing human expressions of anger with emotion-conditioned text-to-speech models, the piece explores how digital systems simulate and redistribute affect.

Polytope Listening Session 1
2025

6 Channel Spatial Sound Installation

Polytope Listening Session 1 is an experimental audiovisual spatial podcast exploring the multifaceted impact of nuclear weapons, intertwining the bombing of Hiroshima and Nagasaki and current global happenings.
